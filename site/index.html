Working with results

Filter by source or period, select a matched spelling, and change the amount of context. Results are grouped by text. Open an occurrence to read the surrounding passage, or export matches as CSV.

Source dates

Dates in the corpus describe different things, including compositions, manuscripts and editions. The period filter uses the earliest year recorded in the archival date field. Consult each text’s metadata before interpreting a date as evidence of language use.

About the corpus

Malay Concordance provides full-text search across the transcriptions preserved in the Malay Concordance Project archive.

Bibliographic information, editorial notes, and original transcriptions are retained with each text. Search indexes are generated separately; they do not modify the source files.

Reading the evidence

The corpus spans different periods, regions, genres, manuscript traditions, and editorial practices. A match refers to a particular archived transcription, not necessarily an original manuscript spelling or a securely dated spoken form. Check the accompanying metadata for each source.
