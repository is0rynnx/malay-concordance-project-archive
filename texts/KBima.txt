Title: Syair Kerajaan Bima
Edition: Henri Chambert-Loir (ed.), Syair Kerajaan Bima , Jakarta: Ecole français d'Extrême Orient, 1982. Naskah dan dokimen Nusantara III.
Manuscript: Cod.Or. 6726, Leiden University Library.
Dates: text: about 1830; manuscript: 1857.
Provenance: text: Bima, composed by by Lukman, the khatib; manuscript: copied by Muhammad Hasan for Mr. Misor [?], a Dutchman.
MCP word count: 8822 words, comprising 1938 verses.
MCP reference scheme: to the numbered stanzas (1-487) and verses of the printed edition.
Editorial notes:
- Chambert-Loir's text is a diplomatic edition based on the unique manuscript. The edition is meticulously annotated, but only a selection of the notes are attached to the MCP text.
Preparation:
- Added: 1999 - April 2000
- A draft digital text was provided by Henri Chambert-Loir. The published text was scanned in Canberra using Recognita™ OCR and checked against the draft. It was then adapted to the MCP format.
