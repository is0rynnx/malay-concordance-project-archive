Title: Syair Kupu-Kupu dengan Kembang dan Belalang
Edition: Jumsari Jusuf, Tuti Munawar, Retnadi Geria & Amin Fukri Hoesin (eds.), Antologi Syair Simbolik dalam Sastra Indonesia Lama , Jakarta: Proyek Pembangunan Media Kebudayaan, Departemen Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan, Republik Indonesia, 1978, part VIII, pp.189-196.
Manuscript: Ml 255(b): Perpustakaan Nasional Republik Indonesia, Jakarta.
Provenance: text: ? Batavia; manuscript: Batavia.
MCP word count: 1451 words, comprising 346 verses.
MCP reference scheme: to the folios (recto and verso, 75v-81r), stanzas and verses of the underlying manuscript.
Editorial notes and bibliography:
- The first Singapore lithograph of this work is dated 1880.
- The text contains words in the Jakarta dialect. The editors have indicated the jawi spelling of some unusual or unknown words. These notes have been included in the MCP edition. However the editors’ notes are not comprehensive.
- In some instances the text has been regularised in accordance with the editorial notes.
Reference:
- H. Overbeck, "Malay Animal and Flower Shaers", Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, 12.2 (1934): 108-148, at 113, with summary at 145-1146.
